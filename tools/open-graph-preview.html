Frequently asked questions

What are Open Graph tags?

Open Graph tags are meta tags in your page's HTML that control the title, description and image shown when your link is shared on platforms like Facebook, LinkedIn and Slack.

What is the best Open Graph image size?

A size of 1200 by 630 pixels (about a 1.91 to 1 ratio) works well across most platforms. Use a JPG or PNG, and keep important content away from the edges because some platforms crop.

Do Open Graph tags affect SEO rankings?

Not directly. They don't act as a Google ranking factor, but a good preview card can increase clicks and shares, which brings more visitors to your page.

Why is my old preview still showing after I changed my tags?

Platforms cache previews. You can refresh them with Facebook's Sharing Debugger or LinkedIn's Post Inspector. Other platforms may take time to update.

Is this preview exactly what each platform will show?

It's an approximation. Platforms change their card designs and cropping over time, so use this to catch problems early and confirm with each platform's own debugger.

Does this tool store my information?

No. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is saved. If you enter an image URL, your browser loads that image directly from its host to show the preview.
