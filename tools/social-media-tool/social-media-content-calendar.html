Frequently asked questions

How does this content calendar work?

Click any day to add a post. Give it a title, choose the platform and status, and add an optional time and notes. Your posts appear on the calendar, and you can edit, delete, filter by platform and export everything to a CSV file.

Where is my calendar saved?

In your own browser, on your own device. Nothing is sent to a server. Clearing your browser data, using private browsing or switching browsers or devices means your calendar will not be there, so export a CSV regularly as a backup.

Does this tool publish posts for me?

No. It is a planning tool and does not connect to any social media account. The Scheduled status is just a label to help you keep track of your own plans.

What do the statuses mean?

Idea is something you might post. Drafting means you are working on it. Scheduled means it is ready and planned for a date. Posted means it is published.

How often should I post?

There is no universal answer. Pick a schedule you can keep up consistently, for example two or three posts a week, then adjust based on what your audience responds to and how much time you have.

Can I move my calendar to a spreadsheet?

Yes. Use the Export CSV button to download all your posts as a file that opens in Excel, Google Sheets and most other spreadsheet apps.
