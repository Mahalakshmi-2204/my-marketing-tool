Frequently asked questions

How does the caption and hook generator work?

You enter a topic and choose a platform, goal and tone. The tool fills your topic into ready-made hook and caption structures, adds a call to action and a few hashtags, and shows you several options to copy and edit.

Is this AI-generated?

No. It uses ready-made templates and runs entirely in your browser. That makes it fast and private, but other people can get similar wording, so treat the results as a starting point and edit them to sound like you.

What makes a good hook?

A good hook is the first line of your post. It makes a clear promise, raises curiosity or names a problem your audience recognises. Keep it short and specific, and try different styles to see what your audience responds to.

Can I use the personal story hooks?

Only if they are true for you. Story hooks are written in the first person as prompts. Change the details to match your real experience, or pick a different hook style.

How long should a caption be?

It depends on the platform and your goal. Platform limits, such as 2,200 characters on Instagram, 3,000 on LinkedIn and 280 on X for standard accounts, are maximums rather than targets. Often only the first line or two shows before the "see more" link, so make the start count.

How many hashtags should I use?

There is no fixed number. A few relevant hashtags usually work better than many. The tool suggests a small amount for each platform, and you can add, remove or swap them for ones your audience actually follows.

Does this tool store my topic?

No. Everything runs in your browser, and nothing you enter is sent to a server or saved.
