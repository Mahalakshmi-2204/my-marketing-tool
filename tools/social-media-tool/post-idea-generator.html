Frequently asked questions

How does the Post Idea Generator work?

Enter a topic, audience and content preferences. The tool combines your inputs with built-in templates to suggest post ideas, angles, hooks and calls to action that you can adapt.

Is the Post Idea Generator AI-powered?

No. This version uses built-in templates and runs in your browser. It does not generate new information with an AI model, so edit the suggestions and verify any claims before publishing.

Which platforms does it support?

You can create ideas for Instagram, LinkedIn, TikTok, Facebook, X, YouTube and Pinterest. The suggestions are starting points; adapt the length and format to each platform.

Can I use these ideas for a business page?

Yes. Enter your business topic and intended audience, then choose a goal such as educating, engagement, promotion or website traffic. Make sure each idea matches your brand and offer.

Does this tool save my topic?

No. The generator processes your inputs in the browser and does not send them to a server or save them.
