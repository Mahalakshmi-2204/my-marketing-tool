Frequently asked questions

What is a meta description?

A meta description is an HTML tag that summarizes a page in one or two sentences. Search engines often show it as the snippet under your title in search results.

What is the ideal meta description length?

Aim for roughly 70 to 160 characters. Google truncates snippets by pixel width rather than character count, so treat 160 as a safe guideline rather than a hard limit.

Is the meta description a ranking factor?

Google has said meta descriptions are not a direct ranking factor. A clear, compelling one can still improve your click-through rate, which helps bring in more visitors.

Will Google always show my meta description?

No. Google often rewrites snippets to better match what someone searched for, using text from your page instead. A well-written description is shown more often when it accurately matches the page content.

What makes a good meta description?

Describe what the page offers in plain language, include your main keyword naturally, and give a reason to click. Make it unique for every page and accurate to the content.

Does this tool store my description?

No. The analysis runs entirely in your browser, and nothing you type is sent to a server or saved.
