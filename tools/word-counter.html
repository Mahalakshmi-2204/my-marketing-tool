Frequently asked questions

How does this word counter count words?

It counts any group of characters separated by spaces or line breaks as one word. A hyphenated word like "well-known" counts as one word.

What is the difference between characters with and without spaces?

Characters with spaces counts every character including spaces and line breaks. Characters without spaces leaves out all spaces and line breaks. Different platforms use different rules, so check which one your limit refers to.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time assumes about 238 words per minute, a commonly cited average for adults reading silently. Speaking time assumes about 130 words per minute. Real speeds vary from person to person.

How do sentences and paragraphs get counted?

Sentences are counted by full stops, question marks and exclamation marks. Paragraphs are counted as lines of text separated by a line break. Abbreviations like "Dr." can make the sentence count slightly high.

How long should a blog post be?

There is no required length for SEO. Write as many words as it takes to cover the topic well and answer what the reader came for, and no more.

Does this tool store my text?

No. The counting runs entirely in your browser, and nothing you type or paste is sent to a server or saved.
